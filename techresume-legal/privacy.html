Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026 · Effective date: August 31, 2026

TechResume ("we", "us", "our") is a native Android resume studio. This policy explains exactly what information the TechResume app collects, why we collect it, how it is protected, and the rights you have over it. We design the app to collect the minimum data necessary and to keep your resume data on your device wherever possible.

01 Data We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide our services:

  • Account data — your email address, used solely for sign-in, subscription verification, and account recovery.
  • Resume content — the resume data, work experience, skills, and documents you create or import (including optional GitHub imports).
  • Subscription status — your plan tier and entitlement state, used to unlock paid features.
  • Feedback and reports — content you voluntarily submit through in-app feedback or AI-content reporting.

We do not collect contacts, photos, precise location, call logs, SMS, or device identifiers for advertising. We do not sell your personal data and never share it with advertising or data-broker networks.

02 Data Security

All data is encrypted using AES-256-GCM encryption. Your resume data is stored locally on your device in an encrypted SQLCipher vault, and is only backed up to your own cloud storage (e.g., Google Drive) when you choose to enable sync. API keys for AI processing never ship inside the app; all AI requests pass through our signed proxy.

03 Third-Party Services

We rely on a small number of processors to deliver the app. Each processes data only to provide its service to us:

  • Google Play Billing — subscription purchases and renewal management. Google handles payment details; we never see your card number.
  • Google Sign-In / Firebase Auth — email registration and sign-in. We receive an OAuth identity token only; we do not request access to your Google contacts or account list.
  • Google Drive (AppData folder) — optional cloud backup, stored in your own Drive and encrypted client-side.
  • DeepSeek API — AI text processing (resume optimization, cover letters, interview prep). Only the resume text needed for a request is sent, and it is used solely to generate your result.
  • Cloudflare — edge proxy and infrastructure for international users.
  • Tencent Cloud SCF — AI proxy relay, quota counting, and trial verification on our mainland-China infrastructure. User content is not persisted.
  • GitHub API — public repository metadata, and only when you choose to import from GitHub.
  • Firebase Remote Config — downloads configuration (for example, paywall test groups). No personal data is uploaded.
  • Google Analytics 4 (Measurement Protocol) — anonymous usage events for the trial funnel, sent directly from your device with an anonymous client ID. Fully disabled when no measurement ID is configured.
  • Sentry — crash stack traces and contextual tags, used solely to fix stability issues. You can turn it off in Android Settings → Privacy → Usage & diagnostics.

We do not share your personal data with third parties for their own marketing purposes, and never with advertising networks or data brokers.

04 Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active, and local resume data remains under your control on your device. When you delete your account, the local encrypted vault is removed immediately and server-side account data is permanently removed within 30 days — or within 60 days for users in the EU/UK exercising GDPR rights and 45 days for California (CCPA) requests, as described on our Data Deletion page. Two narrow exceptions, isolated from our product systems, may outlast those windows: anti-fraud and security audit logs are kept for a maximum of 90 days and then deleted or fully anonymized, and tax or accounting records we are legally required to keep may be retained for up to 7 years. Backup copies held in your connected cloud storage are removed according to that provider's retention.

05 Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data at any time:

  • Access & correction — all of your resume data is visible and editable directly in the app.
  • Deletion — delete your account and all associated data in-app via Profile → Delete account, or request deletion through our Data Deletion page.
  • Export — export your resume as PDF at any time.

06 AI-Generated Content

TechResume includes AI writing features. Content you send for AI processing is transmitted to our AI provider solely to generate your requested result. AI output may contain errors and should be reviewed before use. You can report offensive or inaccurate AI output directly within the chat using the report option, and we review submitted reports.

07 Children

TechResume is intended for professionals and job seekers and is not directed to children under 16 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it within 7 days.

08 International Users

The app is served through regional infrastructure (Cloudflare for international users, Tencent Cloud for users in mainland China). Data is processed according to this policy regardless of region, and appropriate safeguards apply to any cross-border transfer.

09 Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date and, where appropriate, notified in-app. Continued use after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
