Data Deletion

Last updated: August 30, 2026

You can delete your TechResume account and all associated data at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. This page describes exactly what is deleted and the two ways to request it.

01 Delete in the App (Immediate)

The fastest way is directly inside the TechResume Android app:

  1. Open TechResume and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Choose Delete account.
  3. Read the confirmation, then confirm twice — on the final step you type DELETE to confirm.
  4. Your account and data are permanently deleted immediately.

This permanently deletes your account, subscription record, resumes, and job applications on this device and on our servers. This action cannot be undone.

02 Request Deletion by Email

If you no longer have the app installed, or cannot sign in, you can request deletion from our support team:

Send an email to numbtibag@gmail.com from the address associated with your account, with the subject "Data Deletion Request". We respond within 72 hours and complete verified deletion requests promptly.

To help us verify your request, please include:

  • The email address registered with your TechResume account.
  • A clear statement that you want your account and all associated data deleted.

03 What Gets Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following are permanently removed:

  • Your account record and sign-in credentials.
  • Your subscription and entitlement record on our servers.
  • All resume content, work experience, skills, projects, and education entries.
  • Cover letters, interview prep content, job applications, and tracked pipeline data.
  • Feedback and AI-content reports you submitted.
  • The local encrypted data vault on your device.

If you enabled optional cloud backup to your own Google Drive, deleting your account does not remove files already stored in your personal Drive — you can delete those directly in Google Drive.

04 Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription, because billing is managed by Google between you and Google Play. To stop future renewals, cancel your subscription in Google Play before or after deleting your account:

  • Open Google Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, select TechResume, and tap Cancel subscription.

For refund questions, contact numbtibag@gmail.com or request through Google Play per its refund policy.

05 Retention & Timeline

In-app deletion takes effect immediately for your account and server-side data. Email requests are acknowledged within 72 hours and completed promptly after verification. We retain no account data after deletion except where a short retention period is legally required, after which it is permanently removed.
